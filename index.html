Privacy Policy

HerGlow is a self-care and personal-growth app for adults, published by Fluenta Labs. This page explains what the app collects, why, who helps us run it, and your choices. It describes the app as it works today.

Who can use HerGlow

HerGlow is for people aged 18 and over. When you create an account you confirm that you are 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18, and we will delete an account if we learn that its owner is under 18.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (we store only a secure hash of the password, never the password itself), and the time zone of your phone, saved with your profile so the app can follow your local day.
  • Your answers and goals from setup (focus areas, challenges, mood at the time, routine preferences, goals, and how many minutes you have each day if you choose to answer), and your first name if you choose to add it.
  • What you create in the app: your daily plans and what you complete, the items you add to your own plan (their titles, optional times and how often they repeat) and your choices about which routines are suggested, habits and their history, journal entries, mood check-ins and the optional notes you add to them, the guided routines you complete, and your reminder choices.
  • Your optional note: if you choose, you can write one short note (up to 140 characters) in setup or in Edit my plan. It is stored only for you, is not used to build your plan or sent to anyone, and is deleted with your account.
  • Standard connection information: like any online service, our hosting provider records technical logs of requests (such as your IP address and the time) for security and reliability.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone. The app does not currently use advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, build your plan, save your journal, habits and moods, and show your progress.
  • To send the reminders you ask for. Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself and contain no private content.
  • To send you the one-time codes used for sign-up and password reset.

Who helps us run HerGlow

  • Supabase: provides the secure database and sign-in. It stores your account and content. Our database is hosted in the European Union (Ireland).
  • An email delivery provider: sends the one-time sign-up and password-reset codes, so it handles your email address and those messages.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored in a database where each person can reach only their own information. We do not claim that HerGlow is end-to-end encrypted: our servers can technically access stored data, and we limit that access. No system is perfectly secure.

How long we keep data

Your content stays until you delete it or delete your account. When you delete your account, your data is deleted from our live systems immediately; copies in encrypted backups age out within 7 days.

Your choices and rights

You can edit or delete entries and habits at any time, turn reminders off, and delete your whole account (see below). Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to ask for a copy of your data or to correct it, and, in the EU or UK, to complain to your data protection authority. To make a request, write to us at the address below.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

How to delete your account and data

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open HerGlow and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile, Settings, Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and everything in it: plans, habits, journal entries, mood check-ins, goals, reminders and settings.

If you cannot open the app

Email alwaysaihelp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my HerGlow account". We will delete your account and reply to confirm.
